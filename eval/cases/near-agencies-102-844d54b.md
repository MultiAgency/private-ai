## Plan

Two coordinators acting at the same moment can each file a Transfer proposal for one task (both read the treasury before either lands a proposal). The proposals are identical — same payee, amount, token and task URL, exactly what `filedProposal` matches — and approving both pays the task twice. The first cut added the issue's cheap safety net around the approval; the owner's oversight review and ai-review found three gaps in it, which this update closes:

- `lib/payouts.mjs` — the duplicate check no longer waits on the task's recorded proposal to still be `InProgress`. One `duplicatePayoutProblem` audit now reads every matching proposal (same payee, amount, token and task URL) that is still `InProgress` or `Approved`, whatever the recorded one's status, and the approval panel (`pendingPayouts`), the payout sweep (`flagDuplicateProposals`), the close (`closeIfPaid`) and `payout.mjs approve` all read it. A proposal approved in Trezu between sweeps therefore leaves its duplicate flagged and the job open, instead of the job closing over a still-votable extra. The recorded proposal counts from its own live state (not the recent-proposals window), so one that fell out of the last 100 is not mistaken for one that is gone.
- `lib/payouts.mjs` — when exactly one matching proposal is `Approved`, recording adopts it as the task's payment: the ```paid record names that proposal's id and vote transaction, whatever the recorded payout fence says, and the flag asks an approver to reject every other live proposal, the recorded one included. Previously the panel was blocked forever asking for the impossible rejection of the approved proposal, and rejecting the recorded one left the real payment unrecorded.
- Where two or more matching proposals were approved, the task was paid twice: the sweep says so once, loudly, on the task (`**Double payout:**`), records nothing further, and lists the incident under `paid_twice` in `/api/health` so a person can recover the extra transfer.
- `payout.mjs` — `approve` refuses to vote while the check reports a duplicate.
- `lib/payouts.mjs` — the audit's window. The duplicate checks read matching proposals from the treasury's newest 100 only, so once the treasury's counter moved a hundred past a job's proposals, a double payment quietly turned into a recorded payment and the job closed over it, and `paid_twice` stopped being reported — also after a restart, which found nothing to report again. The audit's reads now reach down to the job's own proposals — two coordinators race to file within moments, so a duplicate's id sits beside the proposal it repeats — whatever the treasury's total has grown to, and keep the 100 newest while no payout is recorded.
- `lib/payouts.mjs` — the advice beside a dead recorded proposal. With the recorded proposal dead (expired, rejected, removed) and two matching proposals still live, the flag and the panel told an approver to approve or reject around the dead one — which can never be paid, and `proposePayouts` never files again beside a recorded payout, so the task could never be paid. The advice now names a live proposal as the payment: approve the lowest-id live proposal and reject the rest. Recording already adopts whichever live proposal an approver approves, so the task is payable again once the advice is followed.

Recording still happens at most once per task, and volunteer tasks are untouched: they never file a proposal, so they never enter the audit, and volunteer-only jobs still cost no chain read.

Not done here, per the issue: the locks around payout filing and `/approve`, and dropping AGENTS.md's one-coordinator rule — both wait until the member list moves to shared storage.

## Verification

- `npm ci`, `npm run check`, `npm test`: all pass (410 tests, 0 failures), the same commands CI runs.
- The tests for the gaps, each red before its change and green after (`node --test test/payouts.test.mjs` with the roster fixtures):
  - "an approved recording proposal keeps its duplicate flagged and the job open" — the approve-41-in-Trezu race: the payment records from 41, 42 stays flagged for rejection, the job does not close, and it closes once 42 is rejected; failed while the duplicate check ran only for a recorded `InProgress` proposal.
  - "an approved duplicate is adopted as the payment and the recorded proposal flagged" — the payment records naming 42, the flag says reject 41, and the job waits for 41 to die; failed while recording keyed only to the recorded proposal.
  - "two approved proposals are reported as a double payment and never recorded" — one `**Double payout:**` comment (said once across sweeps), no paid record, the job stays open, and `coordinatorHealth().paid_twice` lists the task; failed while a second approval went unnoticed.
  - "the terminal approve refuses to vote while a duplicate stands" — `payout.mjs approve` exits refusing, before any vote; failed while approve only checked deliverables.
  - "a double payment stays reported however far the treasury has moved on" — the treasury's counter sits at 400, so a newest-100 read no longer reaches proposals 41 and 42; the audit still sees both, nothing records, the `**Double payout:**` flag stands, and a restarted sweep (unchanged board, nothing memoized) reports it again; failed without the window fix — the sweep recorded the payment from 41 and closed the job as complete.
  - "a dead recorded proposal makes a live one the payment to approve" (panel) and "a dead recorded proposal hands its payment to a live one" (sweep) — with the recorded proposal expired beside two live extras, the advice names a live proposal to approve and the live rest to reject, never the dead one; both failed while the advice sent an approver to the dead proposal, which can never be paid.
  - Panel-level: "an approved duplicate becomes the payment and the recorded proposal the rejection" and "a recorded proposal approved beside its live duplicate still holds the panel" (`pendingPayouts`); both failed before.
- The window fix rides on the treasury mock reading `get_proposals` the way the chain does (`from_index` and `limit` in the contract's args); every earlier treasury test passes unmodified under that mock.
- The single-proposal and volunteer flows are unchanged: "one matching live proposal draws no flag", "one live proposal behaves as today", the dead-second-proposal and cross-task edges, and the volunteer-only and mixed-job sweeps all pass unmodified. The first cut's tests hold too: "a duplicate proposal is never the payment that is recorded" still passes — with the recorded proposal 41 voted through while duplicate 42 stands, exactly one `**Paid:**` record lands, naming 41.
